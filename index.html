📜 Game Guide & Keys

W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys: Move character around island.

I Key: Interact with trees (chop wood), speak with NPC, build shelter & ship.

E Key: Eat apples to replenish hunger (+5 hunger per apple).

C Key: Board ship once constructed.

Spacebar: Fire ship cannons during Kraken Boss Fight in Level 2.

X Key: Close active dialogue/text overlay.
