📜 Game Guide & Keys

W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys: Move character around.

I Key: Interact (Chop trees, talk to NPC, build shelter/ship).

E Key: Eat apples to restore hunger (+15 hunger).

C Key: Board ship when constructed.

Spacebar: Fire ship cannons at Kraken in Level 2.

X Key: Close dialogue windows.
